Privacy policy

Your privacy matters. This tool is designed with that in mind.

What we collect

This tool does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. All answers you enter into the questionnaire exist only in your browser session and are never sent to any server, database or third party.

How the tool works

Your responses are used solely to generate your personalised AI prompts within your browser. Once you close or refresh the page all information is cleared. Nothing is retained.

Third party AI tools

When you paste your generated prompts into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other AI tool you are subject to that platform's own privacy policy and terms of service. We recommend reviewing those independently.

Cookies

This tool does not use cookies or any tracking technology.

Email collection and purchase verification

When you purchase this tool your email address is collected at the point of sale through the platform you purchased from (such as Gumroad, Etsy or Payhip). This email is stored securely and used solely to verify that you are a legitimate purchaser and to grant you access to this tool. It is not used for marketing, shared with any third party, or retained beyond what is necessary for access verification. By purchasing you consent to this use of your email address.
